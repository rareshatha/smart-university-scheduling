About Us

We are Artificial Intelligence students from Prince Mugrin University, dedicated to revolutionizing academic management through our graduation project:

Smart University Schedule

Our system leverages Hybrid Optimization Algorithms and data-driven insights to automate the creation of highly efficient, conflict-free timetables. Beyond scheduling, we provide an integrated ecosystem with dedicated portals for Administrators, Instructors, and Visitors, ensuring a seamless and smart experience for the entire university community.

Developed under the expert guidance of our Academic Advisors, this project aims to set a new standard for operational excellence in educational institutions.
